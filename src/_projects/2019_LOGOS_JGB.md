### 2024 — OEM, Mesa Economía Feminista (MEF)
[About MEF](https://x.com/MesaEconomiaFem)

This logo is inspired by the green and purple scarf common in feminist activism, as well such as in marches, events, etc. The design is mainly inspired by the knot of the handkerchief, which represents the union, and united together they represent the joint work around the table (Circle). The abbreviation of the economics table "MEF" was chosen as part of the logo using the "Midfield Rounded" typography slightly modified to make the logo more forceful.
