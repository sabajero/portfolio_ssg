*Jerónimo, Akim and Carlos working on the workshop, 2019*



Aleteo Shakuhachi Project is a project that has been on hiatus since 2020 and was born from the search of three friends in Cali, Colombia, to share the knowledge of the construction, teaching, interpretation and meditation of Shakuhachi and bamboo. One of the objectives is to allow the opening of this instrument in contexts other than meditation.

The project was conceived by Akim Dibbá Angulo Gómez, Carlos Chacón and Jerónimo Gutiérrez Balanta and workshops were effectively carried out in places such as the Universidad del Valle Cali and the Community of Christians of Cali.

![Logo](/assets/images/projects/2019_ALETEO/Shaku_logo.png)
*Logo of the project designed by Jerónimo*

One of the largest workshops we had was a proposal that we developed together with the Community of Cali Christians (movement for religious renewal), they kindly lent us their space to set up our workshop for a few months and offer a 3-week workshop for young people of vulnerable neighborhoods of the city where they would learn to build their own shakuhachi's, in the process we went from cutting, selecting the bamboo, to the construction of the instrument and finally an introductory course on how to play the Shakuhachi flute.

We also had the opportunity to hold a workshop within the framework of the Japanese Culture week at the Universidad del Valle, this was aimed exclusively at the construction, contextualization, uses and interpretation of the instrument in our context.

More pictures from this project can be seen on the official Facebook page: [Aleteo Shakuhachi](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100070093427734)
