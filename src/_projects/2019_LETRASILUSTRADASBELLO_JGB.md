An international call was made to artists, illustrators and cartoonists based on a challenge: they had a period of 24 hours to illustrate a word. The winners of the challenge were selected to illustrate a fragment of the story "Hidden". The result was an exhibition at the Marco Fidel Suarez Library in Bello Antioquia, where the presentation of the book containing all the illustrations of the participants of Letras Ilustradas 2019 was also made. I was exclusively responsible for my illustrations for one of the sections of this book which eventually was presented as an exhibition.
