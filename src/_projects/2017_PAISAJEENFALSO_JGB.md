In 2017, as part of my Projects course in university with Professor Leonardo Herrera Madrid, I carried out this soundscape and sound editing exercise. The idea of this work was to collect sounds from a walk along the busiest street in Cali at that time: The fifth street, and then edit these sounds in Adobe Audition, the entire editing was based on cutting and joining street sounds to simulate the sounds of the night in the green areas of Cali, where crickets, cicadas and frogs predominate, a not so deep approximation of the concept of simulation (Foucault) was that I tried to convey with this work.

This small academic project was part of the Portable Art meeting organized by the University of Los Andes in 2017, at the Casa Obeso Mejía, a subsidiary of the La Tertulia museum, where the "order" of the exhibition was to make works that could be transported and assembled in a single afternoon.
