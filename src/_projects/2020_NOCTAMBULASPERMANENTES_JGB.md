The "Permanent Nightowls" project was initially born as a proposal to do muralism at home due to confinement due to covid-19. Spending more time at home, I became aware of the immense number of beetles, butterflies, moths and other tiny insects that died and remained on the plants every night, so I began to record them with a log and after several exercises I arrived to the idea of drawing them around the lights in my house. The idea behind the work was to reflect on the environmental impact on insect species using drawing and simulation, this is how these bugs appear in graphite, which have their own shadow and weight but do not fulfill their purpose of burning in the light bulb.

The project was pre-selected for the local grant project show "PROGRAMA C DIGITAL – Museo La Tertulia 2021," as a performance and digital educative project however it did not advance to the finals.
